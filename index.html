About me

I have spent more than twelve years working across the infrastructure that keeps organizations running, from Windows Server and Active Directory to virtualization, networking, security, and Microsoft 365. Today, at Manaseer Information Technology, I work on enterprise infrastructure while exploring practical ways to run open source AI locally, including Linux environments hosting large language models with Ollama.

My career began in education and corporate IT, where I worked with servers, networks, virtualization, and security systems. Over time, this led me to Microsoft, where I worked as a Microsoft 365 Identity Support Engineer. My work there focused on Microsoft Entra ID, Conditional Access, authentication protocols such as SAML and OAuth, identity governance, and hybrid identity in enterprise environments.

I have always been interested in how systems work and, more importantly, how to make them reliable. For me, good infrastructure is not just about keeping systems running. It is about building environments that are secure, resilient, recoverable, and manageable when something goes wrong.

Alongside infrastructure administration, I use PowerShell and Python to automate repetitive tasks and improve operational efficiency. More recently, I have been exploring self hosted AI and open source LLMs, with a particular interest in how they can be applied within controlled enterprise environments.

My certifications include Microsoft Azure Administration, Cisco CCNA, and Microsoft MCSE. My interests continue to sit at the intersection of infrastructure, identity, automation, and AI.
