About me

I have spent over twelve years working with the invisible machinery of computing—the directory structures, network perimeters, and virtualization layers that quietly keep organizations running. Currently, at Manaseer Information Technology, I oversee enterprise infrastructure while building out our localized, self-hosted Linux environments to run open-source large language models (using Ollama) securely inside the business.

My path began in education and corporate IT, managing physical server rooms, hypervisors, and FortiGate security firewalls. That curiosity for distributed architecture later led me to Microsoft, where I worked as a Microsoft 365 Identity Support Engineer. There, my days were spent deep in the mechanics of Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), zero-trust conditional access, SAML and OAuth authentication flows, and hybrid directory synchronization for enterprise environments around the world.

I believe good infrastructure should feel quiet and resilient—backups that restore reliably when tested, identity policies that protect high-privilege access without adding friction, and systems designed to withstand unexpected failures. Along the way, I’ve grounded my practice with certifications across Azure Administration (AZ-104), Cisco CCNA, and Microsoft Solutions Expert (MCSE).

When I’m not tuning production systems, I write PowerShell automation tooling and explore the convergence of self-hosted Linux and practical on-premises AI. I am always glad to connect with fellow engineers and builders.
