About me

I have spent more than twelve years working with the infrastructure that keeps organizations running, from Active Directory and network security to virtualization, Microsoft 365, and enterprise systems. Today, at Manaseer Information Technology, I work on enterprise infrastructure while also building and managing Linux environments for self hosted, open source large language models using Ollama.

My career started in education and corporate IT, where I worked across servers, networks, virtualization, and security. This experience eventually led me to Microsoft, where I worked as a Microsoft 365 Identity Support Engineer. There, I focused on Microsoft Entra ID, Conditional Access, authentication protocols such as SAML and OAuth, identity governance, and hybrid identity in enterprise environments.

I believe good infrastructure should be reliable, secure, and largely invisible when it is working well. That means backups that can be restored when needed, identity controls that protect privileged access, and systems designed with resilience and failure in mind.

Alongside infrastructure administration, I use PowerShell and Python for automation and continue to explore the practical use of self hosted AI in enterprise environments. My certifications include Azure Administration, Cisco CCNA, and Microsoft MCSE.

I am interested in the intersection of infrastructure, identity, automation, and AI, particularly where new technologies can be applied in practical and controlled enterprise environments.
